Link Share

Link Share is an site for sharing cool links you find with your friends.

You do this through RSS feeds. RSS is a tool that allows people to receive updates from websites by adding a URL to their RSS reader. Here it is used to give you your own feed of links that they can share with others.

To use RSS you need an RSS reader. I reccomend Inoreader. You should put a widget on your phone's home screen!

RSS works best when posts are not frequent and you read every post. As such, Link Share has a 6 hour cooldown. Nobody finds something actually worth sharing every 6 hours.
